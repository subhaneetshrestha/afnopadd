# Performance results, 2026

SPEC.md success criteria 1 to 4, measured on the shared fixture (`androidTest/.../fixtures/PerfFixture.kt`):
5,000 hand-like strokes of 60 points and 20 images of 1,024 x 768 px over a 20,000-unit square.
The tests are `PerfTest` (criteria 1 to 3) and `DeepZoomRenderingTest` (criterion 4); the tile and
renderer numbers come from `TileLayerTest` and `RendererPerfTest`.

Frame times are the UI thread's cost of recording a frame into a hardware display list; the GPU's
render-thread time is not included. Debug builds run partly interpreted, so these are pessimistic.

## What it took (details in `docs/perf/PERF.md`)

- Tiles (Task 24) for crowded views: the full overview went from 119-138 ms to about 1-2 ms.
- Tiles draw strokes too small to show their mesh as polylines, like the screen: tiles for zoomed-out
  views fill in quickly instead of tessellating every stroke (0.03x median 54 ms → 1 ms).
- Tiles start at 200 visible elements, not the spec's 2,000: a mesh stroke costs the UI thread about
  65 us here, so 330 took 22 ms (0.32x median 22.7 ms → 0.7 ms with nearest-level tiles, 9.1 ms with
  the sharper tiles below).
- Tiles use the level at or above the zoom, so they are only ever shrunk and stay sharp at working zoom.
- Deep zoom (Task 25): beyond 100x a stroke is re-tessellated with a 100x finer epsilon; beyond
  10,000x only its points near the view are kept, rebased near the view and scaled, so floats keep
  sub-pixel precision. Without this a long stroke's cap at 100,000x was 16 px off.

## Known costs not on a frame

- A commit on a 5,000-stroke sheet copies the element map (1.66 ms median) and the next frame re-sorts
  z-order (0.46 ms). That is once per gesture at pen-up, not per frame, so no persistent-collection
  dependency is proposed now; it grows linearly, so revisit past about 30,000 elements (about 10 ms).
