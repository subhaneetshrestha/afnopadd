# Afnopadd

## Specs and tasks

Specs, plans and tasks live under `docs/`, one folder per feature: `docs/features/<id>/spec.md` and `docs/features/<id>/tasks.md`. The project-wide spec, capability map and roadmap (order of work, checkpoints, definition of done) are in `docs/product/`. The layout and the spec and task templates are in `docs/README.md`.

- Follow `docs/README.md` for every new or updated spec, plan or task. This overrides any skill's default locations: never write `SPEC*.md`, `tasks/plan.md` or `tasks/todo.md` at the repository root.
- When finishing a task, update its status, checkboxes and notes in the feature's `tasks.md`, its row in that file's summary table, and its checkbox in `docs/product/roadmap.md`.
